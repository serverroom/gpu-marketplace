# Contributing

Thank you for helping with the agent.

- **License.** Everything you contribute to this repository (code, documentation, issues and comments) is licensed under the MIT License in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), the same terms the project is published under.
- **Sign-off.** Each commit must carry a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <email>` line, which `git commit -s` adds. By adding it you certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): that you wrote the change, or otherwise have the right to submit it under this license. Pull requests whose commits lack the sign-off are not merged.
- **Ideas.** Suggestions in issues are welcome. They are not confidential, and Server Room and its affiliates may use them without payment or credit. If you are a customer, your Terms of Service apply as well.
- **Security.** Report vulnerabilities privately to support@serverroom.net, not in a public issue.
